GymBuddy Privacy Policy

GymBuddy collects your location for its "Gyms Near Me" feature. Gyms Near Me will display some gyms nearby your current location.

GymBuddy does not collect any personal information from you. The location data is used solely to provide the "Gyms Near Me" functionality and is shared with Google via the Google Maps API and Places API. This data is encrypted in transit.

By using GymBuddy, you consent to the collection of your location data for the purpose described above. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the app.

GymBuddy does not store any other personal information about you.
