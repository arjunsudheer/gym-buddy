GymBuddy Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 1, 2026

GymBuddy is designed to work without collecting or transmitting your personal information.

The app stores the workout information you enter, including exercise names, sets, repetitions, weight, workout notes, and selected workout days, on your device. It also stores your preferred weight unit (pounds or kilograms) on your device.

GymBuddy does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising, location tracking, or third-party services to collect your personal information. Your workout data is not sent to GymBuddy or to a remote server.

Your device may include app data in Android backups or device-to-device transfers, depending on your device settings and Google account backup configuration. Those backups are managed by you and your device or platform provider.

You can delete workout data from within the app where available, or remove all app data by clearing GymBuddy’s storage or uninstalling the app through your device settings.

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.
